# Evidence

Priority: live runtime; captured raw traffic; actively served/executed artifacts; current process/container/proxy/startup configuration; persisted state; derived artifacts with provenance; checked-in source; comments and dead code.

Prefer current over stale, direct observation over interpretation, raw over undocumented transformations, reproducible over one-off, and known provenance over unattributed data.

Record: claim, status (`observed|inferred|hypothesis`), evidence, exact input/command, decisive result, reproduction state, confidence, and uncertainty.
